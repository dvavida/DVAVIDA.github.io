The DVAVIDA Manifesto

DVAVIDA — There Are Two of Them.
There Have Always Been Just The Two of Them (Birth & Rebirth).

DVAVIDA is not merely a name. It is a principle. It is the complete expression of the eternal cycle: Birth, Death, and Rebirth.

Where others see an ending, DVAVIDA recognizes a beginning. Death is not the final destination but the threshold of renewal. Every end contains the seed of another beginning, and every beginning carries the memory of what came before.

"DAVID" abbreviation is a broken eternal cycle. It marks Birth and ends with Death. It leaves no place for Rebirth, no path beyond the final breath. It represents a closed circle — a finite existence.

DVAVIDA breaks that limitation. It completes the cycle. The Life is not extinguished by death but transformed through it. Rebirth is not an addition to life; it is the fulfillment of life's eternal nature.

To embrace DVAVIDA is to reject finality. It is to recognize that existence is continuous, that transformation is eternal, and that every ending is the doorway to another beginning.

DVAVIDA is the complete cycle.
Birth. Death. Rebirth.
Forever.
